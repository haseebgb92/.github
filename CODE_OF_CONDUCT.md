# Code of Conduct

We want these projects to be useful places for technical collaboration.

Be respectful, specific and constructive. Discuss the work rather than attacking people. Harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, deliberate disruption, spam and publication of private information are not acceptable.

Maintainers may edit, hide or remove content and may restrict participation when needed to protect the project and its contributors.
